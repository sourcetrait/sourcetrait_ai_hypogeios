The **Hypogeios** project is conveyed to the public as a not-for-profit
iterative demonstration of general software development capabilities.

It makes no claims to any trademarking, derived or originated.


## Derivatives

Hypogeios is a preservation port of classic terminal-based games:
- **Colossal Cave Adventure** => **Speos**
- **Zork** => **Arche** 


### Origin: Colossal Cave Adventure (Open Adventure)

**Hypogeios: Speos** is derived from an authorized preserved port of
**Colossal Cave Adventure**, originally created by Will Crowther in 1976 and
expanded upon by Don Woods in 1977.

The preservation project, **Open Adventure** is authored and maintained by
Eric S. Raymond.

The preserved port project source-code is available at:
[Eric S Raymond / Open Adventure](https://gitlab.com/esr/open-adventure)

The preserved source-code is licensed under:
[BSD 2-Clause](https://gitlab.com/esr/open-adventure/-/blob/master/COPYING)

For general information:
[Wikipedia: Colossal Cave Adventure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colossal_Cave_Adventure)


### Origin: Zork (MITDDC)

**Hypogeios: Arche** is derived from the preserved source-code of
**Zork**, originally created at MIT by Tim Anderson, Marc Blank, Bruce Daniels,
and Dave Lebling in 1977.

Through a series of transfers of ownership, Microsoft eventually held rights
to Zork and released preserved source-code for Zork 1-3 in 2025.

The preserved Microsoft source-code is available at:
- [historicalsource / zork1](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork1)
- [historicalsource / zork2](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork2)
- [historicalsource / zork3](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork3)

The preserved Microsoft source-code is licensed under:
- [zork1: MIT](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork1/blob/master/LICENSE)
- [zork2: MIT](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork2/blob/master/LICENSE)
- [zork3: MIT](https://github.com/historicalsource/zork3/blob/master/LICENSE)

The preserved MIT source-code is available at:
[MIT Libraries Department of Distinctive Collections / zork](https://github.com/MITDDC/zork)

The preserved MIT source-code is licensed under:
[MIT No-Attribution](https://github.com/MITDDC/zork/blob/master/LICENSE.md)

For general information:
- [Wikipedia: Zork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zork)
- [Microsoft: Preserving code that shaped generations: Zork I, II, and II go Open Source](https://opensource.microsoft.com/blog/2025/11/20/preserving-code-that-shaped-generations-zork-i-ii-and-iii-go-open-source)
