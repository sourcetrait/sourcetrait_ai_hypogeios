iterative demonstration of general software development capabilities.

It makes no claims to any trademarking, derived or originated.


## Derivatives

Hypogeios is a preservation port of classic terminal-based games:
- **Colossal Cave Adventure** => **Speos**
- **Zork** => **Arche** 


### Origin: Colossal Cave Adventure (Open Adventure)

**Hypogeios: Speos** is derived from an authorized preserved port of
**Colossal Cave Adventure**, originally created by Will Crowther in 1976 and
expanded upon by Don Woods in 1977.

The preservation project, **Open Adventure** is authored and maintained by
Eric S. Raymond.

The preserved port project source-code is available at:
[Eric S Raymond / Open Adventure](https://gitlab.com/esr/open-adventure)

The preserved source-code is licensed under:
[BSD 2-Clause](https://gitlab.com/esr/open-adventure/-/blob/master/COPYING)

For general information:
[Wikipedia: Colossal Cave Adventure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colossal_Cave_Adventure)


### Origin: Zork (MITDDC)

**Hypogeios: Arche** is derived from the preserved 1977 source-code of
**Zork**, created at MIT by Tim Anderson, Marc Blank, Bruce Daniels, and
Dave Lebling.

The preserved source-code is available at:
[MIT Libraries Department of Distinctive Collections / zork](https://github.com/MITDDC/zork)

The preserved source-code is licensed under:
[MIT No-Attribution](https://github.com/MITDDC/zork/blob/master/LICENSE.md)

For general information:
[Wikipedia: Zork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zork)
