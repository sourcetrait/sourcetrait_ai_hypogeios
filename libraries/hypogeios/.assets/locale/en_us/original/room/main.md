This is a dark chamber of rough black brick, the seam between the worlds. Three *portals* hang in the still air, each glowing a steady color beneath a drifting, half-legible glyph. They stand in the order their worlds were born:
- a [green](#green) portal, marked **σπέος**;
- a [blue](#blue) portal, marked **ἀρχή**;
- a [red](#red) portal, marked **λαβύρινθος**.

Enter a portal by its color.
